About

What shapes my work.

I am a telecommunications engineering student at the National Engineering School of Tunis (ENIT), specializing in Data Science for Intelligent Communications. I am also pursuing a Master of Research in Information Processing and Complexity of the Living. My work spans AI and machine learning, computer vision, optical communications, and intelligent systems. Through research and internships, I have studied world models and JEPA, built a real-time face anti-spoofing system, and developed a Tunisian Arabic lip-reading corpus. I also contribute to IEEE communities in Tunisia.
