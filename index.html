Yosr Jabloun

From signal to insight.

I'm a telecommunications engineering student at the National Engineering School of Tunis (ENIT), specializing in Data Science for Intelligent Communications, and I'm pursuing a Master of Research in Information Processing and Complexity of the Living. My work spans AI and machine learning, computer vision, optical communications and intelligent systems.

I ranked 144th among 950+ candidates in the national engineering school entrance exam. In 2025, I chaired the IEEE ENIT Student Branch, a community of 300+ members, and received the IEEE Tunisia Rising Star Member Award. With my team, I placed second at the IEEE Region 8 Humanitarian Technologies Competition. Through research and applied projects, I've studied world models and JEPA, built a real-time face anti-spoofing system and developed a Tunisian Arabic lip-reading corpus.
